A hand-built canvas flight game, entered in make.fun's [“Make the best flight game” bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/best-flight-game-20261126-0057) on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

Take off at dawn, fly the 8 valley rings in order, mind your fuel and your airframe, and put her back on the runway — your landing grade and leftover fuel decide the score. Fully self-written Canvas2D 3D engine: no assets, no network, just code.

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

then open http://localhost:8080

## Controls

- **Enter** — start / restart
- **W / S** — throttle up / down
- **Arrow keys** — pitch and roll (bank to turn)
- **F** — flaps
- **C** — chase / cockpit camera
- **R** — reset after a crash

Stall if you climb too steep too slow; a hard arrival bends the airframe and costs points. A greased landing with fuel to spare is the way to a top score.
